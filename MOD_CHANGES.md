# Silver Mirror — Custom Mod Changelog

**Base Version:** SM 1.2.1 | **Mod Version:** 8

A comprehensive list of custom quality-of-life improvements, item adjustments, Poké Ball rebalances, and NPC script changes added to this build of *Pokémon Silver Mirror*.

---

## Quality of Life & Controls
* **Dual Registered Key Items:** Assign two key items simultaneously from the Pack menu—**Tap `SELECT`** (`TAP.SEL`) to use the primary item, or **Hold `SELECT`** (`HOLD.SEL`) to use the secondary item.
* **Upgraded Itemfinder:** When detecting a hidden item nearby, the player automatically turns toward the item's direction. If standing directly on top of the hidden item, the player spins in place.
* **EXP Boost Option:** Added an **EXP Boost (`ON`/`OFF`)** toggle in the in-game Options menu to double battle experience points on demand.
* **In-Game Version Display:** Current base and mod versions (`S. MIRROR:1.2.1 MIKEAGAN:8`) are displayed on both the Main Menu and the Save confirmation prompt.
* **Fast Berry & Apricorn Respawns:** Removed the midnight daily reset lock so trees replenish without waiting for the clock rollover.
* **Pokémon Center Healing:** The player automatically turns downward away from Nurse Joy after healing.

---

## Items, Training & Pokérus
* **Vitamins:** Raised the Stat EXP cap from `100` to `200` and increased the gain per vitamin from `+10` to `+20`.
* **PP Up:** Functions like a PP Max, instantly applying all 3 PP Ups to a selected move in a single use.
* **Pokérus Triggers:**
  * **Battle Entry:** Party Pokémon holding a **Nugget** have a chance to contract active Pokérus when entering battle.
* **Wild Held Item Drops:** Wild Pokémon can drop their held items upon defeat.

---

## Custom Poké Ball Multipliers
* **Great Ball:** Increased catch rate multiplier from `1.5x` to `2x`.
* **Ultra Ball:** Increased catch rate multiplier from `2x` to `4x`.
* **Safari & Park Balls:** Increased catch rate multiplier to `8x`.
* **8x Type-Specific Poké Balls:**
  * **Heavy Ball:** `8x` on **Rock**, **Ground**, and **Steel** types.
  * **Net Ball:** `8x` on **Water** and **Dragon** types.
  * **Dusk Ball:** `8x` on **Dark**, **Ghost**, and **Poison** types.
  * **Love Ball:** `8x` on **Psychic** and **Fire** types.
  * **Fast Ball:** `8x` on **Electric** and **Fighting** types.
  * **Level Ball:** `8x` on **Normal**, **Flying**, and **Ice** types (bypassing the vanilla HP scaling glitch).
  * **Friend Ball:** `8x` on **Grass** and **Bug** types.

---

## World, NPCs & Progression
* **Mr. Psychic (Saffron City):**
  * Gifts **TM04 (Calm Mind)** instead of Psychic.
  * Evaluates any party Pokémon across three screens: **DVs**, **Stat EXP** (all 5 stats), and **Hidden Power type**.
* **Oak's Lab Aide (Pallet Town):** Evaluates party Pokémon genetics and offers to reroll any DV under `10` up to `10–15` (and can maximize DVs to `15` for Pokémon that already have solid stats).
* **EXP. ALL & EXP. SHARE Redistribution:**
  * Replaced **EXP. SHARE** with **EXP. ALL** from Oak's Aide at **Route 2 Gate** (paired with the Pikachu Flash dialogue).
  * The Gentleman in **Mt. Moon Pokémon Center 1F** reacts when you buy the Magikarp and gifts **EXP. SHARE** to help train it.
* **Mt. Moon Pokémon Center Updates:**
  * The **¥500 Magikarp** now comes knowing **Bubble**.
  * The newspaper on the table is readable and displays news about Team Rocket.
* **Celadon Cafe:** Added a hidden **Leftovers** pickup.
* **Goldenrod Underground:** Shifted the **Bargain Shop** schedule from Monday morning to **every night**.
